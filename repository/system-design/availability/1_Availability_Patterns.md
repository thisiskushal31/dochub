# Availability Patterns

## What they are

Availability patterns are design approaches that keep a system **operational and reachable** despite failures. They focus on:

- **Redundancy** — Multiple copies of components or data.
- **Fault tolerance** — Detecting failures and routing around them.
- **Recovery** — Failover, restore, and graceful degradation.

Common building blocks: failover, replication, health checks, circuit breakers, and defined RPO/RTO. Use them to reduce single points of failure and minimize downtime.
