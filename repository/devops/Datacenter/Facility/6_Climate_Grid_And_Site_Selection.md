## 1. Concepts

Site selection jobs (global):

| Factor | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Power availability / cost** | MW and lead time |
| **Grid reliability** | Outage culture |
| **Water / heat rejection** | Cooling design |
| **Flood / seismic / wind** | Risk |
| **Fiber routes / cable landings** | Interconnect |
| **Latency to market** | Users/peers/cloud |
| **Permitting / emissions** | Buildability |

Qualify scorecard: [Provider-Use/1](../Provider-Use/1_Qualify_A_Site.md). Build lifecycle: [Markets/2](../Markets-And-Operators/2_How_Operators_Build_And_Run_Halls.md).

## 2. Advanced concepts

### Failure modes

| Mistake | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| Latency afterthought | Wrong metro |
| Ignoring water in desert/ME | Stranded design |
| Power queue blind | Slip years |

### Climate variants

Desert, tropics, Nordics—same Mechanical jobs, different stress ([Mechanical/3](../Mechanical/3_Chillers_Towers_And_Free_Cooling.md)).

## 3. Applications and use cases

| Goal | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| New region | Power+fiber+latency triad |
| AI campus | Liquid+power+grid first |
| Compliance | Residency + physical risk |

**Staff checklist**

- Triad scored  
- Risk register started  
- Never pick only on real-estate price  

**Good:** multi-factor selection. **Bad:** cheap land, no megawatts.
