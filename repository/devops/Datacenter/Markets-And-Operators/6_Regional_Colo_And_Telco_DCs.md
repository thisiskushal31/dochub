## 1. Concepts

**Regional colo** specialists and **telco / cable-landing** data centers often dominate secondary metros and national markets. They may bundle managed services, last-mile, or submarine cable adjacency.

Examples in kind: NTT GDC, many national champions (CtrlS/Yotta-class in India, NEXTDC-class in AU, Teraco-class in ZA, …), and telco DC brands (Singtel, Telstra InfraCo, DT/Orange/BT hall businesses, etc.).

### Why they matter

| Reason | Note |
|--------|------|
| Local compliance / data residency | Often stronger local presence |
| Price / availability | May beat global brands in-metro |
| Cable landing / telco POP adjacency | Latency and interconnect shape |
| Managed services bundle | Ops model differs from pure colo |

## 2. Advanced concepts

### Failure modes

| Failure | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| Assuming global-brand MMR density | Fewer carriers than advertised hope |
| Ignoring telco lock-in | Harder multi-carrier |
| Skipping hands quality checks | Remote ops suffer |
| Not listing in living index | Repeat “who?” in every meeting |

## 3. Applications and use cases

| Goal | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| Secondary metro | Shortlist regional + one global if present |
| Cable-sensitive design | Prefer landing-adjacent campuses |
| Managed vs bare colo | Decide who owns OS/hypervisor |
| Diligence | Carrier list + hands SLA + power path |

**Staff checklist**

- Taxonomy tag in notes  
- Carrier/IX evidence  
- Hands/portal trial ticket  
- Index updated  
- Don’t dismiss regional solely for not being Equinix  

**Good:** local champion + evidence-based interconnect. **Bad:** brand snobbery; untested hands; mystery carriers.
