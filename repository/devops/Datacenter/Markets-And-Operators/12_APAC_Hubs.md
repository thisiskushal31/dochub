## 3. Applications and use cases

| Goal | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| SEA hub | Singapore primary; Jakarta secondary as needed |
| North Asia | TYO/SEOUL per users |
| Australia | SYD + NEXTDC/Equinix-class shortlist |
| AI capacity | Ask liquid/wholesale campuses explicitly |

**Staff checklist**

- Hub purpose clear (IX vs wholesale MW)  
- Cable paths considered for DR  
- Lead times for power  
- Index updated  
- Don’t assume one SG campus equals all SG  

**Good:** metro+building diligence, cable-aware DR. **Bad:** APAC-as-one; ignoring power queues in SG.
